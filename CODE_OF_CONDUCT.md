# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members of our community. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for all those who participate in our community, as well as the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

We invite all those who participate in the AI Engine community to help us create safe and positive experiences for everyone.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:
- GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Code review processes
- Communication channels
- Project documentation
- Community events (online or in-person)
- Any other forums created by the project team

## Our Values

### Be Respectful
- Treat all community members with respect and dignity
- Value diverse opinions and perspectives
- Acknowledge good faith efforts from others
- Accept constructive criticism graciously

### Be Inclusive
- Welcome new members and help them get started
- Use inclusive language
- Avoid assumptions about others' identities or experiences
- Create space for underrepresented voices

### Be Collaborative
- Work together toward common goals
- Share knowledge and expertise freely
- Help others learn and grow
- Celebrate collective achievements

### Be Honest
- Communicate transparently about decisions and changes
- Admit mistakes and learn from them
- Acknowledge limitations in knowledge or expertise
- Keep promises and follow through on commitments

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- **Use welcoming and inclusive language**
  - Use people's preferred pronouns and names
  - Avoid gendered language
  - Be mindful of accessibility needs

- **Be respectful of differing opinions and viewpoints**
  - Not all disagreements are failures
  - Constructive criticism is encouraged
  - Focus on ideas, not individuals

- **Accept responsibility and apologize**
  - If someone has been harmed, take it seriously
  - Apologize sincerely if you've made a mistake
  - Commit to doing better

- **Focus on what is best for the community**
  - Prioritize project health and community wellbeing
  - Recognize that sometimes compromise is necessary
  - Support decisions made through proper channels

- **Show empathy toward other community members**
  - Recognize that people have different backgrounds
  - Understand that technical disagreements aren't personal attacks
- Remember that tone doesn't always come across in text

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- **Harassment and Discrimination**
  - Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, age, race, ethnicity, caste, national origin, religion, or other identity characteristic
  - Unwelcome comments regarding a person's lifestyle choices and practices
  - Deliberate misgendering or use of deadnames
  - Threats of violence or violent language

- **Sexual and Romantic**
  - Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
  - Asking for sexual favors
  - Creating a sexualized environment
  - Sexual jokes or imagery

- **Disruptive Behavior**
  - Deliberate disruption of conversations or activities
  - Sustained interruption of meetings or discussions
  - Aggressive or demeaning tone
  - Trolling or inflammatory comments

- **Bad Faith Engagement**
  - Intentionally spreading misinformation
  - Bad faith arguments designed to derail discussions
  - Attempting to manipulate or exploit community members
  - Spam or repetitive unwanted contact

- **Privacy Violations**
  - Publishing others' private information without consent
  - Taking screenshots of private conversations without permission
  - Doxxing or threats to personal safety

## Consequences

Unacceptable behavior will not be tolerated. If a community member engages in unacceptable behavior, the community may take action, including:

1. **Warning** - First-time minor violations may result in a private warning
2. **Temporary Suspension** - Removal from community spaces for a specified period
3. **Permanent Ban** - Removal from all community spaces for repeated or severe violations

The severity of consequences is determined by:
- Nature and severity of the violation
- Previous violations
- Whether the person took responsibility and apologized
- Impact on the affected individuals and community

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

### To Project Maintainers
- Email: conduct@pyramidxc.com
- Include: Description of incident, individuals involved, witnesses, any evidence
- Timeline: Report within 7 days of incident if possible

### Reporting Process
1. **Initial Report** - Send detailed account to conduct email
2. **Acknowledgment** - We'll acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
3. **Investigation** - We'll investigate thoroughly and confidentially
4. **Resolution** - We'll determine appropriate action
5. **Follow-up** - We'll follow up with reporter on resolution

### Anonymous Reporting
If you prefer to report anonymously:
- Use our anonymous reporting form (link provided in README)
- Contact through a trusted community member who can relay information
- Provide as much detail as safely possible

### Confidentiality
- Reports will be kept confidential
- Only necessary personnel will know identity of reporter
- Reporter safety and wellbeing is a priority

## Special Situations

### If You've Made a Mistake
- Acknowledge the mistake
- Apologize sincerely
- Take corrective action
- Learn from it

We believe in redemption and growth. People are capable of changing and learning.

### Conflicts of Interest
- Moderators should recuse themselves from discussions involving their own projects
- If a maintainer has reported the violation, another maintainer should handle review
- Transparency about any conflicts is essential

### Self-Defense
- Nothing in this Code of Conduct is meant to prevent defending yourself or others
- Standing up to abusive behavior is encouraged and supported

## Appeal Process

If you believe you've been unfairly treated:

1. **Submit Appeal** - Email conduct@pyramidxc.com with:
   - Your name and role
   - Description of the action taken
   - Why you believe it was unfair
   - Any new evidence

2. **Review** - Different maintainer(s) will review your appeal
3. **Decision** - You'll receive written explanation of decision
4. **Timeline** - Appeals should receive response within 7 days

## Community Responsibilities

### Maintainers
- Model expected behavior
- Enforce Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Maintain confidentiality in investigations
- Provide support to affected individuals
- Take reports seriously

### All Members
- Help maintain a positive community
- Speak up about violations
- Support affected individuals
- Assume good faith
- Help welcome new members
